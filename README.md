## About

London based contractor and **"Head of Magic and Wizardry"** (because job titles are overrated).

I deal with the cloud, Kubernetes, microservices, and all the other buzzwords on a daily basis. I am responsible for architecture, development, and maintenance of systems running on the edge of technology.

Most of my code is private (either mine or business choice), but the bits which are public are free for grabs or contributions.
